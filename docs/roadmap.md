- **Contracts rework:** [#227 Contracts panel rework](https://github.com/sekoya-solutions/sekoya-transit/issues/227) is Done: contracts target town needs only and move through four statuses: Proposal, Accepted, Completed, and Failed. Proposals never expire and the deadline starts at the accept date. A new game starts with 3 proposals, then one new proposal appears per month while the list is below its limit; `contracts.json` holds per map size limits (S 6 in the list and 3 accepted, M 8 and 4, L 10 and 5). The panel is about 20% of the screen wide with a colored card per status, the cargo shown by its icon, the cash icon after money, and a new house-shaped population icon (also used in the towns list instead of the word "Pop"). Save format 26 stores the status and settled date; older saves lose their contracts but keep the id counter and generator.
